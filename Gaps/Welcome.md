# Interview preparation notebook

Use **GAPS** to track the concepts you want to strengthen before your next interview.

## A useful revision loop

1. Write the question you struggled with.
2. Explain the answer in your own words.
3. Add one practical example.
4. Revisit the note before your next interview.
