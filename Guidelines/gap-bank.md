## Status Key

- **Not started:** the gap is identified, but no work has begun.
- **In progress:** active learning or practice is underway.
- **Closed:** the evidence column links to a rule, query, lab completion, or write-up.

## Working Rules

1. Review the bank weekly, in the same session as artifact work.
2. Close a gap only when its evidence is recorded.
3. Add new gaps as they come up during labs or real work.
4. Keep open items to about 15 or fewer, so the list stays in use.
