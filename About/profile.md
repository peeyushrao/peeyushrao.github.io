## How I use this notebook

I collect useful references, work through questions, and turn what I learn into notes I can revisit. I aim to explain not just what a tool or concept does, but how it behaves and why it matters during an investigation.

These notes are a living collection, rather than a finished textbook. I revise them as my understanding develops. Queries and commands should be reviewed and adapted to the environment where they will be used.
