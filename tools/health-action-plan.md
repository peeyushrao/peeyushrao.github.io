# Health Action Plan
**Goal: Addressing root causes (CPAP non-use, weight, glucose control)**

---

## 1. CPAP — Top Priority

- [ ] Use CPAP every single night, all night (no skipping)
- [ ] If uncomfortable, book a mask refit/pressure check at sleep clinic — don't just stop using it
- [ ] Ask provider to pull CPAP usage data/report
- [ ] Target: >90% of nights, minimum 4 hrs/night, ideally 7+ hrs
- [ ] Re-check adherence after 4 weeks

---

## 2. Diet

- [ ] Cut refined carbs/sugar: white rice, white bread, sweets, juice, sugary drinks
- [ ] Replace with controlled portions of whole grains (roti/brown rice)
- [ ] Protein target: ~100–125g/day (lean meat, fish, eggs, dal, paneer, curd)
- [ ] Healthy fats okay in moderation (nuts, olive oil, ghee)
- [ ] Fill half your plate with non-starchy vegetables every meal
- [ ] Last meal 3+ hours before bedtime
- [ ] Cut alcohol (worsens sleep apnea + adds empty calories)
- [ ] Track food intake for 2–3 weeks (app-based) to know actual calorie/carb intake
- [ ] Weight loss target: 4–8 kg over 4–6 months (5–10% body weight)

---

## 3. Exercise

- [ ] Walk 30–45 minutes daily, brisk pace
- [ ] Resistance/strength training 2–3x/week (bodyweight is fine to start)
- [ ] Start with even 10 min/day if motivation is low — consistency first, intensity later

---

## 4. Sleep Hygiene

- [ ] Fixed sleep and wake time — even weekends
- [ ] No screens 30–45 min before bed
- [ ] Keep room cool and dark
- [ ] No caffeine after early afternoon

---

## 5. Weekly Tracking (review every Sunday)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| CPAP hours/night | 7+ |
| Weight | Trending down |
| Fasting glucose | Per doctor's target |
| Energy rating (1–10) | Trending up |

---

## Timeline Expectations

- **Weeks 1–4:** CPAP consistency + diet changes → early energy shift
- **Weeks 4–12:** Visible weight loss, better glucose control
