# Introduction to EDR

Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) solutions are designed to continuously monitor and respond to cyber threats on endpoints like laptops, servers, and mobile devices.

## Key Capabilities

* **Detection:** Identify malicious activity using behavioral analysis, threat intelligence, and signatures.
* **Investigation:** Provide context and details about alerts, allowing security teams to understand the scope of an incident.
* **Response:** Enable rapid containment and remediation actions, such as isolating compromised devices or terminating malicious processes.
